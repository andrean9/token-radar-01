Methodology

The model

Implied value = TVL × multiple × momentum × maturity × chains. The multiple is the category’s median market-cap-to-TVL ratio among token-bearing protocols in the snapshot (at least 3 samples, otherwise the global median). Momentum is 1 + 0.004 × the 7-day TVL change, capped at ±50%. Maturity is 0.9 + 0.2 × days since listing, capped at 3 years. Chains is 1 + 0.02 per extra chain, capped at 5 extra chains. The score is the implied value on a log scale from 0 to 100.

Why the median, not the mean

The snapshot’s mcap/TVL ratios are wildly skewed: the global mean is 5.0386 while the global median is 0.2138, and the largest ratio is 400.20. One outlier would dominate a mean. The CEX category shows the same skew: mean 7.8246 against a median of 0.0748. The median is the typical token-bearing protocol; the mean is the outlier.

Sensitivity: what moves the ranking?

The ranking is re-run four times from the snapshot, changing one choice per run. Only the mean-vs-median choice is load-bearing. The other three factors are nudges.

Limitations

  • No shrinkage: a category with only 3 samples uses its median directly.
  • No out-of-sample predictive validation: nothing here is tested against launches that happened after the snapshot.
  • Sensitivity measures stability of the ranking under changed assumptions, not predictive accuracy.
  • Categories with fewer than 3 calibrating samples fall back to the global median.
  • A protocol “records no token” in DefiLlama’s record; that is not proof it has none.
