Methodology

Why the median, not the mean

The model

Each protocol’s implied value is TVL × multiple × momentum × maturity × chains, capped at $100,000,000,000,000 and floored at $1. The multiple is the median mcap/TVL ratio of token-bearing protocols in the same category (at least 3 samples, otherwise the global median). Momentum is 1 + 0.004 × 7-day change, change clamped to ±50%. Maturity is 0.9 + 0.2 × age / 3 years, age capped at 3 years, and 1 when no listing date is recorded. Chains is 1 + 0.02 per extra chain, capped at 5 extra chains. The score is the log-scaled implied value, 0 to 100.

Sensitivity: what the ranking depends on

Each variant re-ranks the board with one choice changed. The mean-multiple variant is the only load-bearing choice; the three factors are small nudges. Sensitivity measures stability of the ranking under changed assumptions, not predictive accuracy.

Limits

  • No shrinkage: a category with exactly 3 samples uses its median directly.
  • No out-of-sample predictive validation: nothing here predicts whether or when a token launches.
  • The snapshot is frozen; a protocol that launched after the snapshot still ranks as tokenless here.
  • Implied value is this entry’s own claim, never make.fun’s.
