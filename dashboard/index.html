    How the ranking works

    Implied value = TVL × category multiple × momentum × maturity × chains. The multiple is the median mcap/TVL ratio of token-bearing protocols in the same category (at least 3 samples, otherwise the global median). Momentum is 1 + 0.004 × the 7-day TVL change, capped at ±50%. Maturity is clamped between 0.9 and 1.1 and rises with age since listing, so an old, large, still-tokenless protocol scores higher. Chains adds 2% per extra chain, capped at +10%. Rows rank by implied value; the score is the min-max normalisation of log10(implied value), 0–100.
